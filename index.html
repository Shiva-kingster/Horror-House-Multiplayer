How To Play

Objective

You and up to 3 other survivors are trapped inside a haunted mansion. Find all 5 keys scattered through the house, unlock the exit door, and escape before the timer runs out — and before the Ghost catches everyone.

Movement

Desktop: WASD to move, Shift to sprint, F toggles flashlight, E to interact/revive, 1-4 to use inventory items, V to talk (simulated radio voice).

Mobile: use the on-screen joystick to move and the buttons to sprint, interact, and use items.

The Ghost

  • Roams the mansion on its own — nobody controls it.
  • It hears movement and sprinting; get too loud and it may investigate.
  • If it sees you, it will hunt you directly. Break line of sight to lose it.
  • It vanishes and teleports unpredictably, cuts the lights, slams doors, fakes footsteps and whispers to unsettle you.

Survival

Watch your Health, Flashlight Battery and Sprint Stamina. If the Ghost catches you, you go Down — a teammate must hold E near you to revive. If every player is down at once, the game ends.

Items

🔋 Battery restores flashlight charge · 🩹 First Aid restores health · 🗝️ Key unlocks the exit (need all 5) · 💣 Flash Bomb blinds/stuns the Ghost briefly · 📻 Noise Maker lures the Ghost elsewhere.
